FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. The core tracker works without registration and stores your information locally in your browser.

Will my data save automatically?

Yes. Tracker interactions are immediately saved using localStorage.

Can I track study sessions?

Yes. Use the 25-minute, 50-minute or custom focus timer.

Can I export my tracker?

Yes. Export the complete tracker as JSON and import it again later.
